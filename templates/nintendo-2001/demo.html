01 — Warm means go

Ration the warmth, keep the chrome cool

The periwinkle chassis and carbon slabs never carry action color. Gold words light the nav, amber fills tools and badges, and signal orange marks every forward cue — arrow discs, chevron chips and Submit. If a panel is warm, it wants a click.

02 — Mobile

One nav bar, the rail becomes a row

The fixed desktop canvas was authored for a single window, so a phone port picks the era's own logic: merge the dual nav bars into one carbon slab with a menu disclosure, stack the panels, and turn the action rail into a horizontal chip row.

03 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the faceplate

Modern soft card 16px radius everywhere · blurred drop shadow · pale gray wash · orange used as decoration. The machine disappears into generic consumer-tech.
Same content, on-style Edge-lit plate with an indigo seam · hard offset shadow · platinum fill · orange reserved for the submit cue. Sharp plates, few pills.
