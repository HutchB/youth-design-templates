01 — The surface ladder

Depth is a step, not a shadow

Every layer is the same near-black ink, separated by lightness and a 1px hairline. Canvas sits under the page, the page under the active row. When a palette floats over the canvas it casts nothing — its lighter surface and visible border do all the work.

canvas#07080a · behind everything
surface#0d0d0d · page, palette
raised#121212 · active, inputs

Hover lightens a row one step; selection lifts it to raised. Both effects are reversible with one property — that is the whole depth system.

02 — Color roles

White is the action, red is the signature

The solid white pill is the only primary CTA — black text on white, no exceptions. Red belongs to the mark and to at most one hero moment per page. Category colors like the yellow below live inside extension illustrations and never on chrome.

primary#ffffff · the CTA
accent#ff6161 · mark, hero
categoryillustrations only
danger#ff453a · destructive

Pressed state dims the pill one notch — the button never changes hue, it only changes how much light it returns.

04 — Mobile

The palette becomes the home screen

On the phone the command bar is not an overlay — it is the app. The search field pins to the top, results run full-bleed at 44px, and the preview pane is gone: tapping a row either fires the command or pushes a detail screen.

05 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the console

Red-tinted card with a glow 16px radius pushed to a colored drop shadow, red text on the body, tinted chrome. Two violations at once: the ladder was abandoned and the brand voltage leaked out of the mark.
Same content, on-style 10px radius, surface fill, hairline border, white text, muted body. It floats because it is lighter than the canvas — that is the entire trick.

Also out: a colored CTA, a light theme, radius above 16px, and any shadow — even a soft gray one.
