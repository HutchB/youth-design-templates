01 — The stripe rule

White speaks, black holds, stripe signs

The primary color is white and it arrives as type and as a 1px outline — the outline is the button. The tricolor exists only to sign things: the 4px divider, the wordmark, a badge. It never fills a surface or a call to action.

Uppercase 700 display at zero tracking, Light 300 body, and 1.5px tracking on every machined label. Shadow stays off the chrome; photography does the depth.

02 — Mobile

Razor edges, stacked figures

The band rhythm survives the phone with one change per element: the headline scales, the spec grid drops to two-up keeping its 32px-style values, and photography stays full-bleed instead of shrinking into margins.

03 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the precision

Consumer-tech panel 16px radius · soft gradient wash · blurred shadow · bold body copy · stripe used as a button fill. Instantly reads as a lifestyle app, not motorsport.
Same content, on-style 0px corners · 1px hairline · flat #0d0d0d cell · Light body · stripe reserved for the signature. The rectangle is the brand.
