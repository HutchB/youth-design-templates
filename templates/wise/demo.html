01 — Type & numbers

Money speaks at weight 900

Headlines run at the heaviest weight the system has, tight and unapologetic. Amounts are equally loud but always tabular, so a ticking rate never shuffles the fee rows below. Every money figure pairs with a currency chip, never a bare number.

02 — Color

Lime on neutral, ink for structure

The sage canvas and white cards carry the mood; the lime button is the only saturated action on screen and always sits on sage or white, never on green. Inputs and the converter draw confidence from full-strength ink borders — pale gray borders read as broken here.

03 — Mobile

The flow becomes one column

Phones strip the transfer to its decision spine: two amount rows, the rate between them, one fee line, one lime button. Supporting cards drop below the fold where they cost nothing.

04 — Off-style, for contrast

What kills the friendliness

Sharp box on dark green 6px corners · lime CTA dropped onto a green surface · generic green button competing with the brand action. Every rule here exists to protect the brand's one conversion moment.
Same content, on-style 24px-radius white card on sage, ink-bordered fields, lime on neutral. Surface contrast does the elevation; the lime button stays the only thing shouting.

Also out: weight-700 hero type, gray input borders, pure-white page canvases, and a second accent color — the palette is sage, white, lime, ink, and a pinch of yellow confetti.
