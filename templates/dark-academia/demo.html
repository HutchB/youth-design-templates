Chapter VII  ·  On Catalogues

The Promise of the Ledger

❦

Every collection begins as an argument against forgetting. The first catalogue was a list of debts; the modern one is a list of promises, and the college has kept both since the founding. To read the shelves in order is to watch three centuries of readers argue with each other in the margins.

The hairline is the college’s favourite piece of furniture. It separates a course from its neighbour, a chapter from its commentary, an argument from its evidence, and it does all of this without ever raising its voice.

Glosse: the “green-shaded lamp” of the Wren Room is not nostalgia — it is the only light in which this parchment reads without glare.

What the ledger asks of the reader is patience, and what it offers in return is continuity: the same hand, the same rule, the same gold line, term after term.

01 — Controls

Oxblood asks, gold decorates

Buttons are small-radius rectangles with small-caps serif labels; the filled oxblood is the only voice that asks for something. Inputs are parchment fields with an ink caret, and focus is a brick rule — never a glow. Gold never appears on a control.

Oxblood marks actions, selection and stamps. Antique gold is ornament — rules, seals, keylines — and never fills a surface larger than a seal.

02 — Mobile

The ledger folds, the rules hold

The catalogue becomes a single column of department chapters. Hairlines and small caps survive intact; only the arithmetic changes — margins tighten, rows wrap, and the one permitted shadow becomes the reserve bar.

Selected · LTR 314
  • One chapter at a timeThe two-column catalogue (main + marginalia) stacks: departments follow each other down the page, and marginalia becomes a divider-separated list at the end. Nothing becomes a carousel.
  • Rows wrap, they do not truncateCourse number and title stay on the first line; tutor and time wrap to a full-width second line indented under the title, so every row still reads top-to-bottom like a ledger entry.
  • Small caps shrink, never vanishDepartment and metadata caps drop from 12px to 9–10px with the same tracking. They are the system’s indexing voice and survive every breakpoint.
  • Targets grow, chrome does notCourse rows become ≥56px tall and the reserve button rises to 30px with wider padding. Hairlines stay 1px; the gold masthead rule stays 2px.
  • The drop cap survivesThe reading sheet keeps its keyline and drop cap, trimmed to ~52px. It is the strongest page-turning signal the system has and costs nothing on touch.
  • Hover becomes tapQuiet text links darken on hover on desktop; on touch they rely on the oxblood selected bar and the reserve bar, both of which are tappable states, not pointer states.
  • One shadow onlyThe floating reserve bar carries the sole shadow-md of the mobile layout; cards keep the faint shadow-sm so the parchment never looks laminated.

03 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the reading room

Modern gradient SaaS card 16px radius · pastel gradient fill · blurred slate shadow · geometric sans · pill button. Any one of these turns the catalogue into generic product UI. Get started
