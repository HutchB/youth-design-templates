01 — Canvas & chrome

Monochrome panels, hairline edges, blue only when something is selected

The workspace is measurable: a 44px toolbar, 26px layer rows, 28px inspector rows and a 24px mono status bar. Panes are flat fills split by 1px hairlines over a dot grid — the chrome stays monochrome so the single blue signal always means selection.

44 / 26 / 24px

Toolbar, layer row and status bar. Fixed heights, never improvised.

1px

Every pane divider, over a faint dot grid on the canvas. No inner shadows between columns.

blue = selected

Layer rows, frames and handles tint in primary at low alpha. Blue does nothing else here.

02 — Color roles

Two anchors, one signal, four gradient anchors

White and near-black carry the whole page. Blue is a signal, never a surface — it marks selection, focus and links. The violet gradient family does the atmospheric work, but only from inside tiles; chrome never picks it up.

Gradient anchors (#d44df0 / #6a4cf5 / #ff7a3d / #ff5577) are inline values on tiles, not tokens — chrome is built from theme.css tokens alone.

03 — Poster typography

Size and tracking carry the hierarchy, not weight

Display type is a geometric grotesk at medium weight with a fixed −5% tracking — the confidence comes from scale, not from going bold. Body is Inter at 15px with its character variants; data and coordinates switch to mono.

04 — Pills, lift & selection

CTAs are pills; selection is a surface lift or a blue ring

✦ ▶ ⌘ MonthlyYearly

Pressed feedback is a slight scale-down, never a darker fill. Tabs and toggles show their active state with a raised lift and hairline edge — never with color. Inputs take a blue-tinted focus ring on dark raised fill.

05 — Mobile

The Phone breakpoint folds, it does not redesign

At 810px the grids collapse; below that the nav folds into a menu and every grid goes single-column. Display type steps down through the tiers while keeping its tracking ratio, and the tiles keep their 30px radius.
