01 — Color roles

Calm gray chrome, one accent, a Mac green

Windows are white, chrome is a single warm gray, and one blue does the pointing. The green is macOS systemGreen — a slightly different value from the iPhone's, one of the visible seams between the platforms. The accent follows the user's system setting, so every hex here is a community-sampled default, not a brand law.

primaryaccent blue · selection

Traffic lights — #FF5F57 / #FEBC2E / #28C840 — belong to window chrome alone; in dark mode every role shifts values together. This theme pins the light column.

02 — Shape & depth

Vibrancy for chrome, a shadow ladder for surfaces

Two materials do all the work. Chrome (menu bar, sidebar) is translucent gray plus a 24px backdrop blur — vibrancy, letting the wallpaper glow through. Surfaces float on a shadow ladder: hovered controls take shadow-sm, popovers and the Dock shadow-md, the window itself a large diffuse shadow-lg. iOS depth is background steps; Mac depth is real elevation.

Menu bar — rgb(246 246 248 / 0.72) + blur(24px)
Vibrancy sidebar — rgb(240 240 244 / 0.8) + blur(24px) The wallpaper glows through; the tint stays gray, never colored.

03 — Controls

Small, precise, pointer-first

Controls run at Mac sizes: 6px corners, 13px labels, compact metrics. The switch is smaller than the iOS one and takes the accent when on — with a green accent it goes green. One filled button per view; everything else is white with a hairline.

04 — Typography

13px body — the Mac runs denser than the phone

The system stack is the identity (SF is not redistributable, so the stack starts with -apple-system). Sizes center on the 13px Mac default, with 11px for sidebar labels and secondary text. Emphasis is weight, not size.

05 — Mobile

The desktop dissolves into the phone idiom

Below desktop sizes the Mac metaphor lets go: no menu bar, no Dock, no traffic lights. The window becomes a full-screen app and the sidebar becomes navigation; what survives is the temperament — quiet gray chrome, one accent, hairline rows.

  • Chrome dissolvesThe menu bar, Dock and traffic lights have no touch equivalent. The window becomes the full screen; launching moves to the home screen and view-switching to tab bars.
  • Sidebar becomes navigationThe vibrant list turns into a pushed detail view with a back chevron. Selection color survives: the detail title keeps the accent treatment.
  • Type and rows grow13px body rises to 15–17px and rows stretch to 44pt minimums — fingers tap where a pointer once hovered. Compact controls relax to their iOS metrics.
  • Vibrancy retreats to barsThe sidebar's blur becomes a solid or near-solid fill for legibility; translucent material remains on navigation bars, as the iOS system does it.
  • Hover becomes pressThe pointer's soft gray highlight becomes a pressed fill for as long as the finger stays down; switches and checkboxes still take the user's accent in one motion.
