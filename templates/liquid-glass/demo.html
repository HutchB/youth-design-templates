01 — Material recipe

Three layers, all of them required

A Liquid Glass panel is a tinted fill (rgba(16,18,26,0.55)) with blur(24px) + saturate(180%), a specular edge — a 1px inset top highlight at 45% white over a faint ground — and a real drop shadow. The tint is the point: color lives in the backdrop, the glass carries a dark film of it. The specular edge is what makes a panel look bent rather than merely blurry.

Flat translucency Fill + blur only, a bare 1px border, no specular inset. It reads as a dim sticker on the light — this is the layer most often forgotten.
Liquid Glass The same fill and blur plus the specular edge (inset 0 1px 0 at 45% white), a faint bottom ground, and the drop. The rim now catches the light field.
  • 1 · tintsurface fill, never above ~70% opacity — the backdrop must roll through
  • 2 · opticsbackdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%) — the boost makes color glow
  • 3 · specularinset 1px top highlight at edge-highlight, ground at edge-ground
  • 4 · dropshadow-md keeps the panel above the light field; hover brightens the border

This is the boundary with the collection's Glassmorphism entry: there the glass is colourless (white at 5–12%) over an ink night scene and tint is forbidden. Here the glass itself is tinted and the backdrop is vivid. Colourless panels over a dark monochrome scene are the other system.

02 — Color roles

The backdrop owns the color; the glass borrows it

Three light fields — violet #2b1e66, teal #0e7a8a, magenta #c2186e — stage the scene as large soft radials. System blue marks active tiles and the primary control; system pink is the rare second signal; cyan is garnish, once per screen at most.

03 — Controls

Everything round, everything glass

Controls are capsules and circles that keep the full material stack — tint, optics, specular edge — even when tinted with a state color. The white thumb and white fills are the moving parts; color marks state, ink stays legible.

On Off

One tilted panel per composition at most; press states scale to ~0.97; focus is a 2px white ring, never a removed outline.

04 — Mobile

The phone is the material's home ground

Control center shrinks the tiles, halves the blur for performance, and keeps the specular edge — it is the identity. The backdrop gets larger and smoother so small panels still have clean light to refract.

05 — Off-style, for contrast

What kills the material

Solid white card

Opaque, flat, no optics — a card from a light marketing theme dropped onto the light field.

Once the panel is opaque it stops being glass: no tint to carry the backdrop, no light rolling through, nothing to refract. The light field behind becomes wallpaper.
Liquid Glass card

Tinted translucent fill, blur + saturate, specular rim, real drop.

The same layout as glass: the scene glows through the film, the rim catches it, and the panel reads as a material instead of a sticker.
Glass without the specular edge

Correct tint, correct blur — but only a bare 1px border, no inset highlight.

It slides into the night-glass look of the Glassmorphism entry: colourless-feeling panels over a dim field. The bent-light signature is gone.
Specular edge restored

Same panel with the inset 45% white rim and the faint ground.

The rim is what bends light; with it, the panel belongs to this language, not the other glass system.

Also out: glass fill above ~70% opacity, flat solid backdrops, nesting glass more than one level deep, radii under 12px, neon tube borders, scanlines, and glowing text.
