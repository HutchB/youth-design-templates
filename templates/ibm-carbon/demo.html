01 — Layering

Three grays and a dark shell

The page is Gray 10, every working surface on it is white with a 1px Gray 20 border, and the shell inverts to ink #161616. Depth reads from these flat steps and their hairlines — shadows are held back for dropdowns and modals.

Selection states may also spend Blue 60 — a selected row is the one place a second blue fill is legitimate. Hover is a one-step gray (#e5e5e5), never colored.

02 — Data table

32px dense rows, checkbox, 3px selection edge

Headers are 12px Gray 70, never all-caps sentences. Numbers are tabular mono and right-aligned. The selected row takes the Blue 10 tint and a 3px blue left edge — structure comes from the grid, emphasis from a single hairline of color.

03 — Feedback & actions

Rectangles, one fill, tinted receipts

Buttons stay rectangles on the 2px radius: one filled blue per view, outlined secondary, ghost for the rest. Inline notifications answer the user with a 3px edge and a tinted background — success green, info blue, danger red.

04 — Mobile

The shell becomes a drawer, the grid keeps its corners

Small screens drop chrome, not discipline: the dark nav folds into an overlay drawer, tiles stack full-width, and the table either scrolls in its wrapper or degrades to stacked rows. Corners stay 2px, type stays 14px, and blue stays an action color.

05 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the engineering voice

Rounded gradient card 14px corners, a blue-to-purple gradient, a glowing colored shadow, display-size type. Any one of these turns a working console into a brochure.
Same content, on-style 2px corners, white fill, 1px Gray 20 border, 14px body. The surface is supposed to be quiet — the data is the loudest thing on screen.
Blue as decoration
Blue headers, blue bars, blue everything When Blue 60 decorates instead of pointing, the one real action loses its pointer.
Blue as a verb Headers stay ink, structure stays gray, and the single filled button is the only wide blue area. Selection may share the tint — decoration never does.

Also out: serif or downloaded fonts, soft shadows inside the page, colorful hover states, and any corner rounder than 4px.
