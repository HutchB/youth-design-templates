01 — Color roles

primary#000000 · text, borders, inverted blocks
surface#ffffff · page & panels
muted#6c3b00 · secondary text only
accent#0000ff · links, selection
danger#ff0000 · errors, one hero block
block-yellow#ffff00 · focus fill, shapes

Three inks max per screen, plus black and white. There is no gray in this system.

02 — Shape vocabulary

Radius is 0 or a full circle — nothing in between. Shapes may overlap and crop off the margin.

03 — Buttons & states

Hover swaps fill and text outright (200ms, colors only) and scales to 105%; active presses to 95%.
Tab to a button to see the yellow focus fill. Disabled is hatched, never dimmed with opacity.

04 — Cards

Exhibit

Flat by rule

Cards are white with a 4px black border. Separation comes from the rule, never from elevation or a tinted fill.

Studio

Shared edges

Cards butt together and share their 4px rules. Gutters between blocks are the exception, not the default.

Archive

Rank by ink

Hierarchy is carried by size and by which ink the tag uses — not by shadow, opacity, or a rounded corner.

05 — Inputs

Focus removes the outline and floods the field with yellow — the loudest available signal, and the reason yellow is reserved for it.

06 — Overlay

Delete exhibit?

This removes the piece and every print derived from it.

The 8px black offset has zero blur. It is the only depth cue this system permits — shadow-sm and shadow-md are none.
