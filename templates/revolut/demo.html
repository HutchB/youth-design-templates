Metal plan

Cashback on everything, a metal card, and priority support — unlock the full wallet.

01 — The white pill

The brightest pixel does the work

On true black, the primary action is a white pill with black text — it needs no color to dominate. Cobalt is a stamp, not a theme: one featured surface per view. Everything else on the screen is luminance, hairlines and white numerals.

Metal · upgrade

If a second cobalt element wants into the viewport, it drops to a raised card — the stamp only works while it is rare.

02 — Dark ladder

Two rungs, plus cobalt

The surface ladder has exactly three steps: true black canvas, #16181a raised cards, and the cobalt featured surface. Depth comes from those luminance steps and 1px hairlines — never from stacked shadows. The gradient lives only on the card mockup, where the physical product earns it.

03 — Mobile

The phone is the native habitat

The shell was born on a phone: balance up top, four quick actions, the card mockup as a hero object, transactions below. Desktop just adds a rail and an analytics column — the dark ladder and the white pill never change.
