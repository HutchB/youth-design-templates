01

Blocks, borders & hard shadows

One outline color (ink), one shadow recipe (offset down-right, zero blur), one label color on fills (near-black). If an element has color, it has a 2px border; if it has elevation, the shadow moves on hover and slams flat on press.

Yell at the roadmap

Q3 planning — bring opinions, leave politeness.

  • One column, full bleed blocksThe three-up feature grid collapses to a single stack; cards keep their 2px border and 4px shadow. The hero art panel drops its rotation to stop shadow-clipping at the edges.
  • Nav becomes logo + burgerThe link row folds into a bordered hamburger block pinned right. The CTA moves to the bottom of the screen as a full-width yellow slab.
  • Targets grow, borders don'tButtons go from 44px to 50–52px tall and badges keep 12px type. Padding grows; the outline stays exactly 2px and shadows stay pure ink.
  • Hover becomes pressTouch has no hover, so the shadow-push state moves to :active only — the block slams flat under the thumb and springs back on release.
  • Type stays loud but scales downHero type drops from 64px to ~40px but keeps its 900 weight and uppercase; body drops to 14px. Never thin the weight to fit.
  • Marquee still dividesThe stripe divider survives as a single line of repeating text, clipped to the viewport — it is the cheapest brand reminder in the system.
03

Off-style, for contrast

Soft modern card

16px radius, hairline border, gradient wash, blurred 24px shadow, slate-gray text at 60% opacity.

Every choice here is polite. In this system "polite" reads as broken — the outline disappeared, the shadow melted, and the color ran away.
Same card, on-style

4px radius, 2px ink border, flat white fill, hard 4px shadow, near-black text at full strength.

The border is visible, the shadow is a shape, and the type shouts. That is the whole material — everything else is decoration.

Also out: gradients and blur of any kind, white text on saturated fills, 1px hairlines as structure, pastel desaturation, and more than one 8px-shadow hero per screen. When everything shouts, nothing is loud.
