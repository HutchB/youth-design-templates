01 — Color roles

Depth is a ladder of tints, not shadows

Every level of the interface owns a container color one step deeper than the last, and each container carries its matching on-color for text. Purple fills the one most important action; the tertiary rose is a single deliberate moment per screen.

surface#fef7ff · page
surface-container#f3edf7 · bars
container-high#ece6f0 · search
secondary-container#e8def8 · cards
primary#6750a4 · the one fill
primary-container#eaddff · FAB
accent#7d5260 · tertiary
danger#b3261e · error

Text on a tint is never gray: #e8def8 pairs with #1d192b, #eaddff with #21005d, #error-container with #410e0b. The container and its on-color move as one token pair.

02 — Shape

Rounder means more important

Shape is a token like color: 8 for small controls, 12 for cards, 16 for the FAB, 28 for sheets and dialogs, full-round for pills, chips and the nav indicator. The bigger the radius, the closer the element reads to the user.

8 · control
12 · card
16 · FAB
28 · sheet
full · chip

Nothing ships with a corner under 8px. A square card is the single fastest way to make this system look like something else entirely.

03 — Components

Four buttons, one FAB, chips that earn their fill

Filled carries the primary action, tonal sits second, outlined and text finish the ladder. Filter chips gain a checkmark and the tonal fill when selected. The switch grows its thumb from 16 to 24 when it turns on.

04 — Elevation & state

Tone first, shadow last, states as a wash

Cards rest flat in their container color. The three low shadows are kept for truly floating parts — FAB, menus, dialogs. Interaction states are an 8% wash of the on-color (12% pressed), and focus is a 3px primary ring.

Restingcontainer color only · no shadow
Hover / pressed8–12% on-color wash over the container
Focus3px primary ring · keyboard navigation

Hover never recolors the text or swaps hues — the wash is always the container's own on-color, so any palette swap keeps working.

05 — Mobile

Built for the thumb first

This interface is Android-native, so the phone layout is the source of truth: targets stay at 48dp, the bottom bar keeps its 80dp height, and the large top bar collapses into a 64dp bar when content scrolls under it.

06 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the tonal language

Hard-edged neo card Square corners, 2px black border, offset hard shadow, white fill. Every choice is readable — and none of them belong to this system, which separates layers with tone and rounds everything from 8px up.
Same content, on-style 12px radius, secondary-container fill, matching on-container text, no border and no shadow. The card is separated by tint alone — that is the whole trick.
Gray text on a tonal container Mid-gray secondary text on #e8def8 fails contrast and breaks the pairing rule — containers always take their own on-color.
On-color pairing #1d192b on #e8def8. If a container changes, its text token changes with it — they are one pair, never mixed à la carte.

Also out: shadows between sibling cards, corners under 8px, more than one tertiary moment per screen, and glossy gradients standing in for elevation.
