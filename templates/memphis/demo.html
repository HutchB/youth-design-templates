01 — Geometry

The confetti set

Six shapes, all drawn sharp as inline SVG: zigzag, squiggle, polka dots, triangle, semicircle, cross. Four to eight per screen, one polka band at most — confetti is air, never wallpaper.

Every line is crisp and constructed — the wobbly hand-drawn crayon line belongs to Doodle Pop, not here.

02 — Colour

Four hues that argue politely

Coral, teal, mustard and violet may meet two at a time; one hue dominates each view. White or ink does the labeling — never a fifth color.

Coralprimary · the loudest block
Tealaccent · the clash partner
Mustardwarm blocks & polka bands
Violetthe wild card, used last

Small text on coral or teal is ink; white on those fills is reserved for large bold display type. Mustard always takes ink.

03 — Controls

Stickers with physics

Interactive pieces get the 2px ink outline and the hard flat offset; hover pulls the piece into its shadow, active flattens it. Static blocks stay flat — the floor is flat.

The poster rolls up

Soft gradients need not apply
