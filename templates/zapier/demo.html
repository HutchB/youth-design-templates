01 — Color

One orange, everything else coffee

Orange carries exactly two jobs: the primary CTA and the live toggle. Secondary actions fall to ink-filled or outlined buttons; there is no second accent hue in the system. App icons — sheets, chats, bases — are the only multicolor moments, and they always sit inside neutral cards.

primary#ff4f00 · CTA + live
surface#fffefb · warm cream
raised#f8f4f0 · cards, wells
ink#201515 · coffee text + fills
the live switch shares the orange duty

Failed runs take a warm red, never orange-red confusion: danger is #d0342c and lives in status contexts only.

02 — Shape

The confident middle radius

Buttons and cards land at 12px — friendlier than a square, more grounded than a pill. Inputs and chips step down to 6px, badges stay fully round, and the important containers draw a full-strength ink border: an editor step card is something you are about to edit, and it looks the part.

6px input 12px card well pill badge

Connectors between steps are dashed ink lines with a circular add button — the line itself explains the product: steps flow down, linked by you.

03 — Mobile

The editor folds into a story

On a phone the zap becomes a vertical story: trigger, connector, actions, with the history panel tucked behind the header. The publish button and live toggle stay pinned where thumbs land.

04 — Off-style, for contrast

What reads as “not zapier”

Pill button, second accent Pure-white card · fully rounded button · a purple CTA competing with orange · cool gray shadow. The temperature, the middle radius and the single-accent rule all break at once.
Same content, on-style Cream well at 12px radius, ink or orange actions, warm shadow. One saturated color does the converting; the neutrals stay warm and quiet.

Also out: pure-white pages, pure-black ink, square-cornered CTAs, orange used as decoration or link color, and cool-gray neutrals anywhere.
