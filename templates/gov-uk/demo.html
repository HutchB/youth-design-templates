01 — Colour

Four working colours and a lot of black on white

Blue is interactive, green is success, red is error, yellow is focus and warning. Everything else is ink on white — body copy stays at full contrast, and the grey fill only marks chrome like banners, panels and hovers.

Every state also carries a word or a shape — "Completed", a tag fill, a 4px red rule — because color alone is never the message, and 1px #b1b4b6 draws every line that needs drawing.

02 — Typography

A small bold ramp on a system stack

Sizes sit on a 16/19/24/36/48 scale. Page titles are bold without decoration, body is 19px at 1.5 (16px on mobile), and emphasis is bold — italic and display faces are left out entirely. GDS Transport stays official-only; the theme uses the system stack.

03 — Buttons & focus

The yellow ring is the elevation system

Buttons are 44px rectangles with a 2px darker bottom edge — the closest thing to depth in the whole system. Focus is drawn the same way everywhere: a 3px yellow highlight with a black outline, over links and buttons alike.

Links keep their underline at rest and thicken to 3px on hover — hovering never changes hue, so nothing shifts meaning under the pointer.

04 — Status & feedback

Words first, then colour

The task list pairs each link with a square tag that says the state out loud. Errors add a 4px red rule above the question, a red border on the field and red text; guidance sits behind a 10px black inset bar; warnings get the yellow.

05 — Mobile

The column narrows; nothing else gives

The service drops to one full-width column with 16px body text, buttons stretch edge to edge, and the task list keeps its tags — they wrap under the link rather than disappearing. Corners stay square and the focus ring stays loud.

  • TypeBody drops from 19px to 16px, page titles from 40px to 28–32px, and line length self-limits — the scale survives, only the steps step down.

  • TouchHover underline thickening has no equivalent; pressed rows and buttons take the grey #f3f2f1 fill for the duration of the press.
  • ErrorsThe error summary jumps to the top of the page and each link in it anchors to the field — on mobile this order matters more, not less.
