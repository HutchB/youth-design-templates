01 — The aurora field

Light as weather, blurred into the night

The field is three radial light fields at low alpha, softened with an 80px blur: violet leading (50%), green answering (34%), pink as a breath (30%). They anchor to corners and edges — never centered, never sharp-edged shapes — and the navy always stays visible around them. This is the depth system: panels don't stack on shadows so much as float in glowing air.

violet leads — behind the hero green answers — lower on the page

At most three fields per viewport, never two hot fields in one corner, and never a saturated full-page gradient — the night must stay navy between the lights.

02 — Color roles

Violet speaks, green answers, pink stays in the sky

Violet is the brand voice — buttons, links, the deepest field. Aurora green is the counter-voice: live states, success, one highlight per screen. Pink belongs to the backdrop first and surfaces as UI garnish only. Secondary text is periwinkle #8b93c7, never gray.

Borders are violet at 25% alpha — structure is faint until an element is active, then border and glow brighten together. Danger is a soft hot red #ff5470.

03 — Components

Glass-leaning panels, ambient halos, quiet type

Cards are translucent steps of the night with hairline borders and a resting violet halo; hover brightens the border and lifts the glow. Buttons are pills — filled violet with a halo, or ghost with a hairline. One gradient-text phrase per page, and glow never touches body copy.

04 — Mobile

Less field, more night

On phones the aurora pulls back: fields shrink and anchor to the top, the nav folds to a logo and burger, cards stack into one quiet column, and buttons go full width. What survives is the calm — vast dark space between few elements.

Deploys that announce themselves

Every release posts a quiet diff before anyone asks.

  • The field pulls backLight fields shrink and anchor to the top corners; blur stays at the token value but the blobs never reach body text. At most two fields on a phone viewport.
  • Nav folds to a burgerLinks collapse behind a quiet icon; the filled CTA survives in the header because it is the page's one violet voice.
  • Cards stack and calm downHalos soften (shadow-sm at rest), gaps tighten to 10–12px, and each card keeps its glyph chip so the three hues still read in a column.
  • Buttons go full width48px pill targets, one primary per screen; the ghost button waits in the menu rather than competing in the column.
  • Hover glow becomes rest glowTouch has no hover, so the resting halo already carries the state; pressed states deepen the border instead of lifting the glow.
  • Type holds its scaleThe hero keeps 30px with its one gradient phrase; nothing shrinks below 13px — space, not size, is what gets cut.
