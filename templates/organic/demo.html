01 — Shape & depth

Nothing in the hedgerow has a right angle

Radii start at 10px and live at 20–28; buttons and inputs are pills; sections break along waves, not rules. Shadows are warm, shallow and overcast-soft — never black, never glowing.

The wave divider is one filled path, two or three gentle bumps, no stroke. A straight 1px rule belongs only inside forms and lists.

02 — Colour

Soil, foliage, clay

Moss leads, terracotta answers, sand and linen hold the ground. If a color could not come out of a garden — neon, purple, electric blue — it does not go on the page.

03 — Controls

Pills, patience, warm hover

Buttons lift 2px and deepen their shadow over 160ms; nothing snaps, nothing glows. Fields are white pills with an oat border and a moss focus ring.

04 — Mobile

The market folds to one stall

Cards stack single-column, the hero keeps its wash and wave, and the stamp steps aside — spacing absorbs the smaller screen, not ornament.

  • One column, cozy gapsThe three-card row stacks with 16–20px gaps; cards keep their 24px radius and resting shadow so the stall-table feel survives.
  • The wave flattens, never straightensHero and section dividers compress from 72px to ~26px tall but stay curves; a hard horizontal rule would break the grown logic.
  • Stamp steps asideThe rotated badge hides under 760px — one ornament per screen still holds, carried by the wave and the chips.
  • Type softens, stays roundedHeadings drop 60px → 24–28px in the same rounded humanist voice; no switch to a neutral corporate face.
  • Targets go full pillsButtons and inputs stretch to 48–52px full-width; the moss focus ring doubles as the visible keyboard affordance.
  • Hover becomes pressThe lift-and-deepen hover becomes a quick 100ms press (translateY 1px) on touch; state never depends on hover alone.
  • Bottom bar carries navigationNav links move to a tab bar with the active item in moss; the sticky header keeps its slightly opaque linen and shadow-sm.
