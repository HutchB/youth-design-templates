01 — Violet atmosphere

Every gray leans purple

The sea is #1f1633 and everything floats on it: raised rows, trace panels, chips. Borders, shadows and secondary text are all lavender-tinted — a neutral gray anywhere reads as a foreign object. Pure black is banned; the purple cast IS the brand.

#1f1633 → #2a1e42

The whole depth story: page to raised island, one purple step, plus a tinted ambient shadow.

1px

Border weight, always in border purple #362d59. Never gray, never black.

3px

Selected-row edge: an inset primary bar that survives every density mode.

02 — Severity colors

A small strict palette for how broken it is

Severity is the one place hue carries data: red F3 fatal, orange E2 error, yellow W1 warning, blue I0 info — always as uppercase chips on low-alpha fills. Red is strictly for real failures; lime pops once per section for the state worth celebrating.

Sparklines follow the severity of their row — a trending fatal burns red, a quiet warning stays purple.

03 — Controls

Chunky buttons, inset press, uppercase labels

⌘K jump to E resolve I ignore A assign

The filled button runs 13px radius with an inset top-light and bottom-shade — it should feel like a physical key. White is the loudest CTA and appears only in marketing moments.

04 — Mobile

The stream survives; the trace collapses

On a phone the issue card loses its sparkline and counts first, keeping chip, title and mono culprit. The stack trace becomes an expandable section inside the issue, and the filter row shrinks to two chips plus a level menu.

What breaks the vigilance

Neutral dark card 6px radius is fine, but the surface is #0a0a0c with gray #3a3a3f borders and gray text — flat, hueless, anonymous. Drop it into any app and nothing would look wrong. That is the failure.
