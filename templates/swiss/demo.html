01 — Typography

One sans, four voices

A single grotesque carries everything: 900 for display, 700 for structure, 400 for prose, and a tracked uppercase layer for meta. Flush left, ragged right; the red numeral does the pointing.

02 — The grid

Structure you can see

The 12-column skeleton may be drawn: faint ink columns behind the page, red numerals on the edges. Content snaps to column edges or gutters — the 8/4 split below is exact, never decorative.

123456789101112

Asymmetric but aligned: the red block lands on columns 10–12 because that is where the grid puts it, not because it looks neat. A dropped column beats a stretched one.

03 — Color & controls

Ink, white, one red

White is the ground, ink does the work, red is the stamp. Controls are sharp rectangles that swap fill on contact — no fades, no lifts, no depth.

Redprimary · the only color
Inkforeground & border
Whitesurface · the ground
Plateraised · image panels

The accent token is ink by design: the grid and the display type are the accent. A second hue would dilute the single voice red has.

04 — Mobile

The poster folds, the grid stays

Twelve columns collapse to four, the display headline steps down without changing its voice, and the fact column becomes hairline rows under the headline. Nothing centers; nothing rounds.

  • Columns 12 → 4The skeleton redraws at four columns; the faint lines and the red edge numerals survive, so the structure stays visible at any width.
  • Display steps, never swapsThe headline goes 124px → 30–34px and keeps 900 weight, uppercase, tight leading and flush-left alignment. Centering it "for mobile" ends the system.
  • Fact column becomes rowsThe 8/4 hero split stacks: hairline fact rows move under the headline in the same order, red indices intact.
  • Index rows go full-widthRow titles wrap to two lines rather than truncating; the red numeral holds its 34px column so the rag stays aligned.
  • Targets grow, rules stay 1pxButtons rise to 44px full-width; hairlines stay exactly 1px ink and radii stay exactly 0.
  • Hover becomes tapThe instant fill swap reads as a press on touch; nothing on the page depends on hover to be operable.
