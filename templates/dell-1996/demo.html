01 — Frame, tints & bevels

Hard edges do all the work

One black frame wraps the page, one ink draws every hairline, and depth comes from hand-style bevels instead of shadows. The tint family gives each product line its own block of color; nothing ever blends, fades or rounds.

Radii are zero and shadows never blur. A "shadow" is a hard 2px offset; a "highlight" is a 1px white edge. If it could not be cut from a GIF, it does not ship.

02 — Mobile

The frame thins, the stack begins

A table-era page on a phone keeps every rule and changes only the arithmetic: the 8px frame becomes 2px, the two columns collapse into one, and the icon grid reflows from two-up to a single row that scrolls. Nothing gains radius; nothing gains a blur.

03 — Off-style, for contrast

What breaks the catalog

Modern soft product card 12px radius · gradient wash · blurred drop shadow · sans body copy. Any one of these turns the page into a costume of itself.
Same content, on-style 1px black border · flat sage tint · Times body · hard rectangle photo with a 2px offset shadow. The frame, the tints and the serif carry the period.

Also out: new hues beyond the tint family, a second red panel on one page, soft shadows, rounded buttons, and sticker art flattened into backgrounds.
