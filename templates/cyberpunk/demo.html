  • Hero becomes a postcardThe sunset band keeps its gradient and grid floor but drops to ~110px tall with a 20px headline; coordinates and kicker fold into the venue card below it.
  • Three-up cards stack, one stays litCard rows collapse to a single column; the "lit" card keeps its pink border and glow, the rest stay dim until tapped. Vertical rhythm tightens to 10–12px so the column still reads as a list.
  • Nav becomes a glowing tab stripThe top nav folds into a bottom tab bar: inactive tabs are muted 10px caps, the active tab is pink with a text-shadow halo. No colored pill backgrounds — the glow is the state.
  • Targets grow, glow doesn't spreadButtons hit 48px tall full-width; chip rows wrap instead of shrinking type. Glow radii stay the same — crowding two glows on a small screen turns the page into haze.
  • Hover fills become instant tapsTube-fill hover states collapse to :active on touch; the pressed button flips to dark ink on neon with no transition, so the state change reads as a switch, not a fade.
  • Scanlines stay, at lower alphaThe fixed scanline layer keeps full-bleed coverage but drops opacity (~5% black) so 13–14px body text stays legible on small glass.

Off-style, for contrast

Pastel soft card

18px rounded corners, pink-cream wash, soft diffused shadow, low-contrast rose text, pill button.

Wrong city: pastel daylight, curves everywhere, and depth done with shadows instead of light. The neon never gets to glow against it.
Same card, on-style

4px corners, violet-black glass, 1px pink tube border, zero-offset bloom, high-contrast ivory text.

Dark air for the glow to live in, square-ish tubes, and exactly one light source — that is the whole night city.

Also out: rainbow palettes, glow on body text, sunset gradients on buttons, white text on neon fills, corners above 8px, cast shadows, and more than one blinking element — the night stays electric, not frantic.
