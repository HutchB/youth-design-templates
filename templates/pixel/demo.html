Cartridge controls

Every control is a flat cartridge fill with a 4px ink border and a solid block shadow. Pressing translates the element onto its shadow and collapses it — the sprite flattens into the page. Bars are bordered tracks of hard segments; menus select with a ▶ cursor glyph, never a floating highlight.

The phone is the handheld

On a phone the RPG screen stops pretending to be a desktop window and becomes the console: the HUD compresses to a 2×2 grid, the command list becomes thumb-sized full-width rows, and the dialog hugs the bottom of the screen like a text box in a portable game.

  • HUD compresses, never hidesFive readouts fold into a 2×2 grid of 3px-bordered cells at 11–13px type. HP/MP bars drop to 4 segments each but keep hard segment boundaries — the bar never becomes a smooth fill.
  • Menu rows become targetsCommand rows grow from 38px to 44–48px tall, stay full-width bordered strips, and keep the ▶ cursor glyph. The selection is also the pressed state on touch.

  • Borders thin one step onlyPanels drop from 4px to 3px borders to win back space; nothing goes below 3px, and the shadow blocks stay solid ink at 2–4px.
  • Slots grid stays squareThe inventory keeps its 4-across square grid down to 320px, then 3-across. Slot counts stay corner digits; slots never stretch into rectangles.
03 · Off-style, for contrast
03

What breaks the cartridge

Modern soft card

16px rounded corners, hairline gray border, blurred 30px shadow, pastel gradient pill button, proportional sans type.

Curves on the grid, light doing depth's job, and a typeface with no pixels in its past — the sprite becomes a sticker.
Same card, on-style

square corners, 4px ink border, solid block shadow, flat cartridge fill, bold mono uppercase.

Flat fill or ink, block or nothing — the grid stays honest and the buttons still feel like they want to be pressed.

Also out: gradients, blur, fades and tweens; white text on cartridge fills; 1px hairlines; gray borders; more than three cartridge colors per screen; and any smooth easing that makes a sprite act like it has a physics engine.
